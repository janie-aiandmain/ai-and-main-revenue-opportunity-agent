# Accounts, privacy, costs and support

PREVIEW. Customer-use terms, support commitments and the customer data-processing
arrangement must be finalized before sales. This is not an executed agreement.

## Your accounts and data

Your CRM remains the system of record. In the current architecture, authorized
note text and timestamps are sent to the configured OpenAI model for evaluation;
supported voice recordings are sent for transcription. Finding/approval/operation
metadata is kept in the isolated application's storage. No account password or
API secret belongs in the setup worksheet, downloaded plan, chat or support email.

Before customer use, verify the actual provider terms, data location/retention,
access and deletion rules for the deployment. Do not assume that synthetic-demo
testing establishes those commitments or a zero-retention service. The current
audit default is 12 months, configurable by the deployment administrator; voice
draft/transcript handling has a separate 24-hour lifecycle. Your CRM's own record
retention is separate. Do not upload regulated or sensitive customer data into
the preview. No background processing starts when you complete the setup plan.

## Price and ongoing charges

The existing proposed DIY offer is $395 once available. It is not activated for
checkout. Payment does not include or imply unlimited CRM, model, transcription,
hosting or email-provider usage. Required subscriptions, API availability and
ongoing charges must be disclosed before purchase. AI & MAIN must choose and
publish who pays each provider, what support/updates are included, and the refund
and license terms. No new price or service guarantee is created by this preview.

## Getting help

Support contact: janie@aiandmain.ai. Share the platform, a safe error code, time of
the problem and a non-sensitive operation reference. Do not send tokens, cookies,
JWTs, customer transcripts or screenshots of credentials. No response-time promise
is finalized. If a CRM save is uncertain, preserve the existing operation and ask
for reconciliation; do not save again or delete evidence to make the error disappear.

## Before buying

Confirm your CRM/plan and note source are supported, the setup is manageable
without coding, current limitations are acceptable, the test has passed in the
intended account, ongoing charges are clear and the support/license/refund terms
are available. If the connector is not released, use a no-charge compatibility
review or waitlist. Do not pay for an unavailable ready-to-use connector.
