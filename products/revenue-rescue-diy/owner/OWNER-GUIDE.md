# Revenue Rescue: your owner guide

PREVIEW — November 19, 2026. The customer package is being prepared. No live
customer connector is released yet. This guide describes the intended released
workflow; your setup worksheet does not connect or change your CRM.

## What it helps you do

Spot customer requests and retention concerns buried in service notes, see the
original evidence, and decide what your team should do next. Your existing CRM
keeps your customer history. Revenue Rescue supplies the finding and review step.

One customer can have several independent needs. Pricing for an EV charger and
a complaint about completed work must stay separate, with their own evidence,
recommendation and decision. A finding is a review item, not a new CRM record.

## The first setup should feel like this

1. Choose HubSpot, Jobber or GoHighLevel. The chosen connector must be available
   for your plan and supported workflow before payment is offered.
2. Sign in and authorize the supported connection through its secure setup flow.
   Never paste an API key, password, recording or customer details into a worksheet
   or ordinary email. The guided connection/provisioning flow is not built yet.
3. Choose the supported note source and name the person who will review findings.
   The current technical preview evaluates one designated synthetic test record;
   it does not scan all your contacts or jobs.
4. Run the supplied synthetic demonstration before allowing real customer data.
5. Review the source, proposed next step and restrictions. If a released action
   is supported, approve its complete final preview before it can run.

The owner-facing setup should not require coding or Cloudflare administration.
These are acceptance criteria for the product, not steps you must perform today.

## Using findings

Read the dated source evidence. Check the exact service, quantity, job status,
price conditions and any restrictions. Historical words such as "today" or "now"
must not become a new deadline. Older interest may need internal verification.

Choose PASS, FAIL or CONCERN for the specific proposal when asked for semantic
review. That decision assesses the wording and evidence. It does not approve a
CRM action. A changed proposal needs review of the revised wording.

An internal verification task asks your team to check records. Pricing preparation
asks your team to prepare the requested price information. Neither authorizes
installation, a visit, billing, an appointment or contacting the customer.

## Optional voice input

The tested HubSpot demo supports recording a service provider, reviewing and
correcting the transcript, confirming the original time and contact, approving
the complete note, then saving it. This preserves the provider's report; it is
not a direct customer recording. A transcript can contain a spoken mistake, so
check quantities and words such as "no" before approving.

Voice-note saving is demonstrated in AI & MAIN's synthetic HubSpot account. It
is not released for customer accounts, Jobber or GoHighLevel. Saving a source note
and approving any resulting task are always separate decisions.

## What a successful action means

A prepared proposal has not run. An approved proposal still needs execution.
A confirmed CRM task or note has a remote ID and a matching receipt. If the
result is uncertain, reconcile the existing attempt; do not create another one.

An opportunity amount is an estimate. A created task is follow-up activity.
Revenue is verified only from separate evidence of an actual financial outcome.
None of these steps guarantees sales or establishes "revenue rescued" by itself.
